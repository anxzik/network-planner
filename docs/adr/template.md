## Context

What forces are at play. Written so someone who was not there can follow it —
name the constraint, not the preference. Cite evidence: files, commits,
measurements.

## Decision

What was decided, in active voice. "We will ..."

## Consequences

What becomes easier, and what becomes harder. The second half is the part worth
writing down — a record listing only benefits is advocacy, not a decision.
